Read the numbers honestly

  • Engagement = likes + shares + favorites, as provided in the dataset. Counts, not rates — big spend buys big counts.
  • Spend is shown as the dataset's modeled range (low–high) for the ad. It is an estimate band, not an audited invoice.
  • CTR and retention are shown as provided. Retention is the dataset's mean retention measure — treat it as relative, not absolute.
  • Top-engaged slice deliberately oversamples the most-engaged ads. It is not what a typical TikTok ad looks like — the representative slice is.

About this data

  • Creatives and metrics come from the course TikTok ads dataset (thousands of ads with video, hydrated on the CU GPU server).
  • Sampling is seeded and documented, so any student can reproduce the slice.
  • Videos are the real ad creatives, compressed for the web. Posters are frames from the actual videos.
