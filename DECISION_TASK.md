# Thursday Decision Task — Adobe Express (instructor key)

## Student-facing task (also in the dashboard Decide view)

Plan media for **Adobe Express** (not in the dataset; deliberate). CMO goal:
new app installs from US creators/small-business owners; $50,000 test budget.
Decide (1) test TikTok or not, (2) which campaign objective to buy. Evidence
only from the studio slices, plus the CPM benchmark table.

## Expected inference (the resounding yes)

1. **Find the competitor.** Canva ("Canva: Design, Photo & Video") sits in
   Games & Apps × App Install: **$0.031 per engagement, 27% CTR, 990,561
   engagements on $30,630 modeled spend**. DoorDash Driver in the same cell:
   $0.023. The category buys installs cheaply here.
2. **Rank objectives.** App Install is the cheapest objective in the sample
   ($0.034/engagement vs $0.130 sample average; ~74% cheaper). Video Views
   second ($0.068). Product Sales and Reach most expensive ($0.168–$0.169).
3. **Check the interaction.** Games & Apps × App Install cell: $0.034,
   n=6, median CTR 16.5%, retention 12.0%. Cheap and on-goal, not merely
   cheap: engagement here is a download-intent proxy for a free design app.
4. **Price the alternative.** $50,000 at the cell's CPE ≈ 1.47M engagements;
   the same money at e.g. $32 local-TV CPM buys ~1.56M *impressions* with no
   install mechanism, and premium video (~$46.50 field note) buys ~1.08M
   impressions. Denominator discipline: TikTok number is engagements,
   CPM number is impressions — students must state this explicitly.
5. **Verdict.** Yes, test TikTok, buying **App Install** — the inference is
   competitor-anchored (Canva/DoorDash prove the cell), not category faith.

## Counterexample students should surface

- **n=6 cell.** Small sample; treat as a test rationale, not a guarantee.
- **Retention is weak** (Canva 6.2%; cell median 12.0%): people engage fast
  and leave; creative must front-load the install ask.
- **Modeled spend.** Tier-calibrated estimates (~$30.6k top tier), not
  invoices; relative rankings carry the argument, absolute CPE less so.
- **Engagement ≠ install.** No install counts exist in the dataset; the
  honest claim is cheap qualified intent, pending an install-tracked pilot.
